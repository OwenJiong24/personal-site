## What I set out to do

Build a card showing two versions of text the CEO typed by hand: the corrected standard version (default) and his original typing (opt-in). 2026-11-04.

## What I built, and the mistake

The toggle was CSS-only - a checkbox and ":checked ~", no script. For the language pair I reused one component and put the **raw Chinese text into the English dictionary** as the "original" panel. Result: the English home page carried his unproofed Chinese, including two typos and the figures $1.5M and 0-5M/Month (see [the profile entry](/essays/2026-11-04-owen-jiong-profile-rev2/)).

Failure count: 1 leak, 2 pre-existing ones found by the same check - `/uses` rendered Chinese on every English item, and `/404` was a Chinese page for every visitor.

## The fix and the gate

- English pages carry translations only; the original stays on `/zh/`.
- The canonical/hreflang gate now fails a build when a non-`/zh` page contains a 20+ character CJK run or a raw-only token. First run: 4 reds. All four were real; none was a false alarm (the `/404` page is the single documented exemption).

## What to copy

Any bilingual component: decide which language sees which text **in the data model**, not in the component. Bilingual data carries both fields at once (`nameEn`, `whyEn`).
