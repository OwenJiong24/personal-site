## Do this (30 seconds)

Make the last line of your instruction: **"When done, all of these must be true: (1) (2) (3)."** Three items max, each one checkable by eye.

Measured on this site, 2026-11-04:
- the card task with the acceptance line "typos invisible by default" went through in 1 round;
- the same task without it cost 2 reworks (one per language);
- of 3 tasks I counted without acceptance lines, 2 needed an extra round naming the language surface.

## One trap

**Don't write "make it good" or "make it pretty"** - the model invents a standard and performs against it. Write facts you can see: word count, whether a failure item is present, which button is highlighted by default.
