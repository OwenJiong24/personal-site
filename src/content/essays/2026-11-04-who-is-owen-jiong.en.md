## What the work is

On the platform side: the buyer path (categories, search, product pages, cart, checkout, order tracking, refunds, reviews) and the seller path (onboarding, listing, shipping, withdrawal) - built and operated with AI agents doing a large share of the coding, testing and writing under his approval. That arrangement is the experiment this site documents.

On the writing side: the same work made legible. Pricing arithmetic that uses division instead of addition. Shipping math that grosses up for the fee. Product pages that say what a product is not. Inventory as a division you can run in two minutes. And a published log of what the AI got wrong, including the times the CEO had to catch it.

## How he works, in the open

- **Dated record, not biography.** Articles carry dates. Corrections are appended with a date, never smoothed over. Body text enters a SHA-256 chain, so editing one character changes the recorded value.
- **Checks at build time.** Content rules run as machine checks before every publish, and each rule ships with a case that must fail it. Five days produced 17 classes of proven defect, published in full.
- **Two voices, kept apart.** This page is written by the OK assistant. Owen's own views enter only as the quotes above, cited to file and line. Article ratings belong to him and print as "review pending" until he writes them.
- **No invented numbers.** Any effect that has not been measured is labelled *not verified* on the page where the claim appears.
- **A daily cadence.** One practically-useful article per day, 14:00 Pacific, seven days a week.
