- Chain head as of 2026-11-04: **52 entries**, printed in every page footer.
- Recomputation from public material: the source is at **github.com/OwenJiong24/personal-site**; `node scripts/verify-chain.mjs` fetches each entry's raw file from `raw.githubusercontent.com` and recomputes the head. It returned **PASS 52/52** on 2026-11-04.
