**Conclusion first: verified profession cards = 0. Method first, labels never.**

The Human Atlas baseline has 26 profession cards, 8 career paths and 24 career stages — but its green ≈80% / yellow ≈50% / red ≈30% / black top-5% figures are self-labelled "experience-based estimates". This site does not treat that kind of number as fact, so all of them are marked unverified here.

## What a checkable claim looks like

1. **Source**: which document, which page, does the URL open?
2. **Sample**: who was observed, how many, in what context?
3. **Boundary**: applicable conditions plus at least one counterexample.
4. **Self-check**: what can the reader verify themselves?

If a claim fails any of the four, it does not go on the page as fact.

## Explicit non-goals

No judging any real person's IQ, health, personality, morality or future behaviour; no ranking by nationality, ethnicity, gender or disease; no AI-generated quantification dressed up as individual measurement; no substitute for therapy, medicine, law or hiring assessment.
