**Conclusion first: this library offers structure, not conclusions you can copy.**

The Thought Atlas organises 2,500 ideas into L1 (base archetypes), L2 (theory families) and L3 (concepts). Each card carries a mechanism, applicable conditions, boundary counterexamples and a lookup entry. The set came from one batch structure pass — **individual source verification is not done**, so every card here says unverified.

## What one card looks like

Take `T01-000 · Critical rationalism`: the mechanism field currently reads "to be confirmed: needs an independently distinguishing mechanism"; conditions cover argument validity and sources of knowledge; the boundary says validity and truth must be judged separately. These are **fields still to fill**, not finished claims.

## The self-check (more useful than the conclusion)

For any idea, ask three things:

1. **Mechanism**: how does it work? "Because it matters" is not an answer.
2. **Conditions**: under what assumptions? Does it survive a change of context?
3. **Counterexample**: can you name one case where it fails?

If you cannot answer, keep it as a lead — not as a fact.

## About the full dataset

The 2,400 L3 cards are not in the page; they are provided as a downloadable data file. The page shows only the 100 bilingual L1+L2 cards. Missing content is left out rather than invented.
